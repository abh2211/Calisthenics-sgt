    Before you register

    Worth knowing

    I can barely do a push-up. Is this for me?

    We all started there, so don’t let that stop you. This programme is open to beginners.

    Do I need previous experience or a certain fitness level?

    Your experience, current training and goals are discussed with Annur on the follow-up call, before you commit.

    When do the sessions run?

    The block starts on Wednesday 25 November at 6pm. Annur will confirm the full schedule when you speak.

    Can I join after the block has started?

    No. Everyone joins at the beginning of the 8-week block.

    What if I miss a session?

    Nothing has to get in the way. A missed session can be rescheduled as a one-to-one session, at a cost of £15.

    What happens after I register?

    Annur will get in touch to talk about the programme and arrange a follow-up call. Your experience, availability and suitability can all be discussed then.

    Anything else, including injuries or health conditions, age requirements, gym membership, what to bring, and cancellation or refund terms, will be covered with Annur before any payment or commitment.
